Why Brown Noise for Pump Noise? Heat pumps, AC compressors, and circulation pumps emit continuous, low-frequency acoustic energy (typically between 30 Hz and 120 Hz) that easily penetrates party walls and floors. Brown (Brownian) noise naturally exhibits a steep 6 dB/octave attenuation curve, providing dense low-frequency power without the fatiguing high-frequency hiss found in white or pink noise.
